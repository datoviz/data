# Gaia and 2MASS Planet Sky

## Source

- Gaia DR3 gaia_source through the official ESA TAP service.
- NASA Photojournal PIA04250, Two Micron All Sky Survey.

## Processing

- Selected 8000 bright Gaia stars by G magnitude with BP-RP colors.
- Reprojected the 2MASS Hammer all-sky map to a continuous equirectangular texture with an inset that excludes the source artwork border.
- Oriented both celestial layers to Earth at 2026-07-16T11:01:00Z using GMST.

## License And Attribution

## Notes

- The 2MASS layer is an infrared false-color survey view, not naked-eye visible light.
- Celestial distance is display-only; angular positions and orientation carry meaning.
