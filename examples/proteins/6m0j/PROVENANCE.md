# 6M0J Protein Arcball Bundle

## Source

- Downloaded source: `https://files.rcsb.org/download/6M0J.pdb`.
- Structure entry: PDB ID 6M0J, https://doi.org/10.2210/pdb6m0j/pdb.
- Structure publication: Lan J., Ge J., Yu J., et al. (2020), “Structure of the SARS-CoV-2
  spike receptor-binding domain bound to the ACE2 receptor,” *Nature* 581:215–220,
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2180-5.

## Processing

- Generated with `tools/preprocess_protein.py` into the C viewer bundle format.
- The prepared directory contains raw `.f32`, `.u32`, `.u8`, and `.rgba8` arrays.
